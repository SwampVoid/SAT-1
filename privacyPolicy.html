Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy outlines how the Face Detector Smart Camera System (“the System”, “we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information. By using our application or prototype interface, users (“you”, “your”) acknowledge and agree to the practices described in this document. The System is designed to provide a preview of a future smart security camera using AI‑powered facial recognition to detect authorised and unauthorised individuals. Although this project is a prototype, we treat user privacy with the same seriousness as a fully deployed product.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information essential for the functionality of the prototype. This may include: Account Information: Username, password, and email address for login and authentication. Facial Recognition Data: Images or facial features voluntarily provided by the user for testing authorised‑user detection. Camera Feed Data: Live camera footage used solely for real‑time detection of authorised and unauthorised individuals. System Usage Data: Basic interaction logs (e.g., login attempts, detection events) to ensure proper functioning. We do not collect unnecessary personal information, nor do we request sensitive data beyond what is required for the prototype’s core features.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes: Authorisation & Access Control: Facial recognition data is used to determine whether an individual is authorised to enter. Security Alerts: If an unauthorised individual is detected, the system triggers an alert within the app. App Functionality: Login details ensure secure access to the app’s UI and features. Performance & Testing: Usage data helps evaluate the effectiveness of the prototype. We do not use your data for marketing, profiling, or external analytics.

4. Data Storage & Protection

To protect your privacy, the System follows these principles: Local Processing: All facial recognition and camera data is processed locally on the user’s device. No Cloud Uploading: Your facial data, camera feed, and login details are not uploaded, shared, or transmitted to external servers. Secure Storage: Any stored data (e.g., authorised faces) is encrypted and kept only within the app environment. Limited Retention: Data is retained only for the duration necessary to operate the prototype. Users may delete their data at any time.

5. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or disclose your personal information to: Third‑party companies Advertisers External AI systems Government agencies (unless legally required) Your data remains entirely within your control.

6. User Consent

By using the System, you consent to: The collection of essential personal information The processing of facial recognition data for authorisation purposes The use of camera feed data for real‑time detection Users are informed of all data collected and may withdraw consent by discontinuing use or deleting stored data

7. Recording & Ethical Considerations

The System only records individuals who enter the camera’s field of view on private property. Entering private property implies consent to being recorded for security purposes. We commit to: Avoiding unnecessary data collection Informing users of what data is stored Ensuring ethical use of facial recognition technology Preventing misuse or unauthorised access to personal data

8. User Rights

You have the right to: Access the personal data stored within the app Request deletion of your data Understand how your data is used Decline to provide facial recognition data (though this may limit functionality)

9. Contact Information

For questions or concerns regarding privacy, data handling, or security, please contact the project developer directly.As the project evolves, this Privacy Policy may be updated. Any changes will be clearly communicated within the app or project documentation.
